HOW TO INSTALL FOLDERS

1. Drag "Folders" onto the "Applications" folder in this window.
2. Open Folders from Launchpad (or press Command + Space and type Folders).


"APPLE COULD NOT VERIFY FOLDERS…"?

This appears the first time only, because Folders isn't registered with
Apple yet. The app is safe: all of its code is public on GitHub.

1. Click "Done" on the warning (not "Move to Trash").
2. Double-click "Can't open Folders? Open me" in this window and click
   the green button (allow your browser to open System Settings).
   System Settings opens at Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll to the bottom and click "Open Anyway" next to
   "Folders" was blocked to protect your Mac.
4. Enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID), then click "Open Anyway" again.

Folders now opens normally, every time.

Tip: the "Open Anyway" button shows for about an hour after the warning.
If you don't see it, open Folders once more, click "Done", and try again.
