Allow Folders to open

macOS blocks apps that aren't registered with Apple yet. Folders is safe (all its code is public), and you only need to do this once.

Open Privacy & Security
  1. Click the green button above. If your browser asks to open System Settings, click Allow.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of Privacy & Security.
  3. Next to "Folders" was blocked to protect your Mac, click Open Anyway.
  4. Enter your Mac password (or use Touch ID), then click Open Anyway again.

Don't see "Open Anyway"? It shows for about an hour after the warning. Open Folders once more, click Done on the warning, then click the green button again.
